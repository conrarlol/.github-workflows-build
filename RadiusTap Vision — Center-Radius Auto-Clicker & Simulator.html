How Center-Radius Lock Works

  1. 1 360° Polar Ring Scan: Samples pixel colors along the purple ring radius to separate the pink outer arc from the white center zone.
  2. 2 Centroid Angle Lock: Computes the exact midpoint angle (θ_target) of the white center blocks immediately whenever the target spawns or relocates.
  3. 3 Predictive Tap Trigger: Calculates angular velocity (ω) and fires a tap at the target's center radius coordinate when Δθ ≤ ω × lead_time.
